    Designed to feel like a call, not a test

    • Two minutes, once a week, at a time Meena picks.
    • No quiz questions. Saathi asks about people, not memory.
    • Saying "not today" is always fine and is never reported as a problem.
    • Audio is not kept. Only names and contact counts are logged.

      Honest limits and how the design handles them

      RiskMitigation
      Not a diagnosis. Social and speech signals are not clinically validated.Output is "change from your own baseline". The last step is a referral to a professional, never a label.
      Natural shrinkage: bereavement, a move, illness.Meena can mark someone as "passed away" or "moved". That person leaves the baseline and no alert fires.
      False alarms scare families.Persistence window of several weeks, a human coordinator confirms before rung 5. This demo compresses time.
      Surveillance feeling.Meena owns her map, chooses what is shared, and can delete everything.
      AI replaces human contact.Success metric is human contacts per week. Saathi's job is to hand over to people.
      Digital exclusion.Works on any phone through voice calls. Family can help set up. Language chosen by Meena.

      Principles

      • The older person owns the data and the map.
      • Family sees counts by default. Names only if Meena says so.
      • Opt-in for every passive signal, one at a time.
      • Delete everything at any time.
